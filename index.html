🦅 The Visionary Strategy

Born on November 9, 1877, in Sialkot, Dr. Allama Iqbal was a premium philosopher, global poet, and critical political thinker. His powerful conceptual framework re-energized the Muslim world with deeper spiritual and intellectual insight, calling for self-awareness, dignity, and collective renewal. He remains a timeless symbol of courage, knowledge, and cultural pride.

📚 Historic Landmarks

  • Ultimate Titles: Shair-e-Mashriq (Poet of the East), Hakeem-ul-Ummat.
  • Literary masterpieces: Bang-e-Dara, Bal-e-Jibril, Zarb-e-Kaleem, Javid Nama.
  • National Honor: November 9th stands as a regular public holiday across Pakistan to protect and remember his legacy.
